A FEW QUESTIONS

Before we connect.

What does David speak about?

Digital humans, conversational AI, facial animation, digital identity and the creative and ethical questions around these technologies.

How can I invite David to an event?

Email the event name, location, dates and the format you have in mind. Include the audience and topics you would like to explore.

Where can I see his film credits?

David’s IMDb profile is linked in the credits section. His career spans facial animation and performance capture across film and realtime experiences.

Which Mimic company should I contact?

For commissioned digital humans, character production and performance capture, start with Mimic Productions. For the AI avatar platform and its plans, explore Mimic Minds.

Can I try the AI avatar platform?

Yes. Visit Mimic AI Studio through Mimic Minds to explore the available tools, plans and deployment options.

Is David available for interviews?

For interviews, podcasts and editorial enquiries, email a short outline and your proposed timing.
