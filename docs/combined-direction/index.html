DAVID BENNETT

The Futurist

David Bennett is a futurist and technologist with over 30 years of experience in visual effects, motion capture, XR, AI, and digital doubles. A pioneer in facial animation, he has worked on films like Avatar, Tintin, and Rise of the Planet of the Apes, and collaborated with artists such as Lady Gaga, Beyoncé, and Kanye West, as well as brands like Warner Brothers, Nike, and Adidas. David has 2 provisional patents, one in AI and one in robotics.

Currently, he is focused on developing realtime conversational AI avatars with applications across various industries, including education, training, medical care and well-being.

David actively contributes to the global AI dialogue, speaking at events like the AI for Developing Countries Forum at the UN, SXSW, AWE and EU AI policy roundtables.

Humanoid robotics
& transhumanism.

David’s interest in robotics grew while working on Beowulf, where he oversaw motion capture for Anthony Hopkins’ character. Studying Hopkins’ face led him to imagine recreating human expression through animatronics and to file a provisional patent application for robotic skin.

Mimic Productions was initially envisioned as a robotics company. Its work expanded into digital humans, performance capture and AI-driven interactive characters, while David continued exploring synthetic human likeness.
