From AI Avatars to Agentic Digital Humans: David Bennett at Agentic AI Summit Berlin

David Bennett explained the shift from AI avatars to agentic digital humans at Agentic AI Summit Berlin on 15 September 2026.

By David Bennett18 November 20261 MIN READ

What changes when an AI avatar can understand context and act?

15 September 2026, 14:30–15:00 | The Ritz-Carlton Berlin | Speaker

David Bennett spoke at Agentic AI Summit Berlin in a session titled “From AI avatars to agentic digital humans: The next interface for human-AI interaction.”

The session examined the shift from text-based chat interfaces towards embodied systems that can speak, respond, remember context and interact in real time.

What is an agentic digital human?

An agentic digital human combines a visual character, conversational AI and a system that can take context-aware actions. It is not simply a face placed over a chatbot.

Speech, facial animation, gesture, memory, turn-taking and safeguards need to work together. The character also needs a clearly defined role so people understand what it can do and where its limits are.

The interface has to feel coherent

Visual quality is only one part of the experience. Low latency, natural timing and consistent behaviour matter just as much. If the voice, expression and response do not align, the interaction loses credibility.

David connected these requirements to Mimic’s work in real-time characters and conversational avatars, showing how character production and AI systems meet in a practical deployment.

Where agentic digital humans can be useful

The format is relevant when people benefit from a visible, social presence. Examples include entertainment, training, customer experience, guided information and immersive environments.

The character should support a real task. A clear use case, transparent behaviour and appropriate human oversight matter more than novelty.
