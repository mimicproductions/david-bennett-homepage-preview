The Human Within the Machine: David Bennett’s IFA Berlin 2026 Keynote

David Bennett presented “The Human within the Machine” at IFA Berlin’s 2026 Kick-Off, with a live Mimic digital human demonstration.

By David Bennett18 November 20261 MIN READ

What makes a digital human feel understandable, responsive and human?

30 June 2026 | Futurium Berlin | Keynote and Mimic demonstration booth

David Bennett delivered the keynote “Mimic - The Human within the Machine” at the IFA Berlin 2026 Kick-Off at Futurium Berlin. Mimic Productions also presented a live demonstration at its booth.

The keynote explored how digital humans are changing from visual assets into live interfaces that can listen, speak, gesture and respond in real time.

The human layer in an AI system

People do not experience an AI system only through its underlying model. They experience its timing, voice, expressions, movement and behaviour. A digital human gives those elements a visible form.

That visible form can make an interaction easier to understand, but it also creates responsibility. The character should be clearly identified as digital, stay consistent with its intended role and avoid suggesting abilities it does not have.

From keynote to live demonstration

The Mimic booth showed how the ideas discussed on stage can work in practice. A production-ready digital human requires character design, animation, speech, conversational logic and real-time rendering to operate as one system.

A live demonstration also exposes the practical details that edited videos can hide, including response time, turn-taking, facial timing and the stability of the complete pipeline.

Why IFA was the right setting

The IFA Kick-Off brought media, consumer technology leaders and innovators together before IFA 2026. It gave David a setting to connect advances in AI with the way people will actually encounter digital characters in products, services and public experiences.
