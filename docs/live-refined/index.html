The Futurist

“In the realm of digital humans and AI, imagination knows no bounds, and the future is ours to craft.”

My career spans more than 30 years in visual effects, facial animation and performance capture, including work on Avatar, Tintin and Rise of the Planet of the Apes.

Today, I lead Mimic Productions and Mimic Minds, bringing that character craft into real-time AI conversations, immersive experiences and new forms of human expression.
